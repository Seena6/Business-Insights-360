# Business-Insights-360
# Overview
Business Insights 360 is a comprehensive Power BI dashboard designed to empower AtliQ Hardware with actionable insights across Finance, Sales, Marketing, and Supply Chain. This repository houses the project files and documentation.

# Tech Stack

• 📊 Power BI Desktop – Main data visualization platform used for report creation.
• 📂 Power Query – Data transformation and cleaning layer for reshaping and preparing the data.
• 🧠 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Used for calculated measures, dynamic visuals, and conditional logic.
• 📝 Data Modeling – Relationships established among tables (resorts, snow, and data_dictionary) to enable cross-filtering and aggregation.
• 📁 File Format – .pbix for development and .png for dashboard previews.

# Features

# Business Problem
AtliQ Hardware is growing fast, but their expansion into Latin America ended in a major loss because decisions were made using limited surveys and basic Excel analysis. As the company has scaled, Excel is no longer enough to manage or analyze their data.
At the same time, their main competitor, Dell, is gaining a strong advantage by using a mature data analytics setup to understand customer behavior, market demand, and regional trends more effectively.
To avoid repeating past mistakes and to stay competitive, AtliQ has decided to build a proper data analytics system. The goal is to bring transparency into their data, support data-driven decisions, and help the company make smarter, more accurate business moves going forward.

# Goal of the Dashboard

The Business Insights 360 Dashboard delivers one consolidated analytical platform that:

Gives executives a holistic, 360° overview of business performance.

Provides drill-downs into Finance, Sales, Marketing,Supply chain and Executive.

Helps decision-makers compare performance vs Last Year and Target Benchmarks.

Highlights opportunities for revenue growth, margin improvement, and cost optimization.

Simplifies data exploration with filters (Region, Customer, Category, Year, Quarter, YTD/YTG).

Finance View: Detailed financial analysis, including revenue, expenses, and profit margins.
Sales View: Insights into product and customer performance.
Marketing View: Analytics to enhance marketing strategy.
Supply Chain View: Optimization metrics for efficient supply chain management.
Executive View: Metrics critical for top-level decision-makers.

# Data Sources

The dashboard seamlessly collects and utilizes data from two primary sources:

Excel/CSV Files: Targets, Market Share data, and related information are sourced from [Excel/CSV files]().
